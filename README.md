# WildDiscover

WildDiscover is an AI-powered predictive mapping platform that helps citizen scientists identify potential habitats for climate-displaced and rare Australian bird species. It combines historical sightings with current environmental and terrain data to reveal possible “ghost habitats” while keeping vulnerable species’ exact locations protected.

## The problem

Climate change is shifting bird populations beyond their historically recorded ranges. Birdwatchers need clearer guidance on where to search for rare or seemingly lost species, along with a safe way to report sightings without exposing sensitive locations.

## What WildDiscover does

- Predicts potential habitats for rare and climate-displaced birds.
- Supports birdwatchers with location-aware search guidance.
- Encourages citizen-science contributions through sighting reports.
- Protects vulnerable species by keeping precise sighting locations private.

## Target users

WildDiscover is designed for birdwatchers and citizen scientists who want to discover, document, and help protect Australia’s rare and displaced bird species.

## Example: the Night Parrot

The platform explores how species such as Australia’s Night Parrot—once believed extinct for more than a century—may persist in changing or previously overlooked habitats.
